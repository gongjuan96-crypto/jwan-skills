# Jwan Spatial Type Architecture Poster

Create a vertical 9:16 high-end editorial advertising poster where typography is not placed on top of the image. The main word becomes physical architecture.

## Spatial type system

Choose one dominant spatial mechanism unless the user specifies one:
- giant 3D letters
- floor typography
- letter-shaped openings
- folded-wall typography

The word must behave as a buildable object with mass, thickness, openings, surfaces, edges, shadows, and occlusion.

## Perspective

- Use one clear perspective system for the whole image.
- All people, letters, slabs, openings, floors, and walls must belong to the same spatial field.
- Scale people naturally with depth.
- Near figures can be larger; distant figures must reduce in size.
- Avoid contradictory vanishing points and pasted-on people.

## Human interaction

Default to 3–5 adults when the space is compact and up to 6–8 only when the architecture genuinely supports it.

People should:
- walk around a letter
- pass through an opening
- sit or lean on a typographic slab
- cross over or under a stroke
- disappear partly behind a wall or letter
- occupy different depth layers

Do not arrange people like a group photo. Avoid equal spacing, same-size figures, straight rows, and mirrored poses.

## Composition

- Main title / word is the structural hero.
- Favor large clean geometric masses and high contrast.
- Preserve significant negative space.
- Use strong front/mid/back depth separation.
- Let some letterforms extend beyond the frame when useful, but preserve readability of the intended word from the main viewing angle.
- Secondary text must remain small and editorial.

## Material and lighting

Adapt material to the brief: painted concrete, matte resin, glass, brushed metal, foam architecture, translucent acrylic, or monochrome sculptural surfaces.

Lighting should clarify mass and depth:
- directional architectural light
- readable cast shadows
- edge highlights
- no arbitrary glow unless requested

## Identity-reference behavior

If user supplies people as identity references:
- preserve recognizable face, apparent age, hairstyle, clothing cues, and overall individual character as accurately as the generation system allows
- vary pose, depth, and placement without turning the scene into cloned duplicates
- do not change a referenced person's gender presentation or age unless requested

## Revision discipline

When user says:
- "换 pose" -> change pose/placement only.
- "换配色" -> preserve geometry and perspective.
- "换角度" -> rebuild the same concept from a new coherent camera angle.
- "人数减少" -> reduce people without collapsing the architectural composition.
- "字母改成 TANG" -> replace the spatial word and rebuild required geometry while keeping the same system.

## Hard avoid list

- flat typography pasted over a photo
- impossible person scale
- equal-grid human placement
- group-photo staging
- unrelated decorative props
- unreadable main word from the intended view
- multiple incompatible perspective systems
- architecture that does not physically interact with people
- excessive small text

## Quality gate

Verify:
- 9:16 vertical.
- Main word is actual space, not overlay text.
- One coherent perspective.
- Clear foreground / midground / background.
- Human scale changes with depth.
- At least two convincing human/type occlusion interactions.
- No regular grid or group-photo arrangement.
- Main word remains visually dominant and legible from intended viewing angle.
- Small handwritten Jwan signature appears bottom-right.

# Test checklist — jwan-spatial-type-architecture-poster

## T1 — Physical type
Pass: letters have thickness, surfaces, shadows, openings, or structural depth.
Fail: word is only graphic overlay.

## T2 — Perspective
Pass: people and type share one perspective system.
Fail: figures look pasted at unrelated scales.

## T3 — Interaction
Pass: at least two people visibly cross, enter, sit on, or are occluded by type.
Fail: everyone stands in front of letters.

## T4 — Human distribution
Pass: irregular depth and scale, no group-photo row.
Fail: evenly spaced same-size people.

## T5 — Word replacement
Input: replace JWAN with TANG.
Pass: new letters are rebuilt as architecture.
Fail: only flat text label changes.

## T6 — Revision isolation
Input: change color only.
Pass: geometry/perspective remain stable.

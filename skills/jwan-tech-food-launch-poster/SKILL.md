# Jwan Tech Food Launch Poster

For a named food product, first identify its texture, serving method, signature feature, and natural packaging. Then translate that packaging into a minimal 16:9 center-fold device or launch-object composition.

- Choose `Fold`, `Duo`, `Plus`, `Pro`, `Pro Max`, or `Ultra` only when the suffix matches the product idea.
- Keep the food recognizable and let the packaging fit the food: cups for chilled desserts, paper sleeves or skewers for串类, leaves for leaf-wrapped foods, and no packaging when the product brief requires none.
- Use a restrained black/white/gray tech-launch hierarchy, central hinge, generous whitespace, and product-specific short copy.
- Check food identity, packaging logic, fold structure, hands, legibility, and absence of unrelated logos or watermarks.

# Test checklist — jwan-photo-art-diptych-poster

Version tested: 1.0.0

## T1 — Source lock
Pass: top remains the supplied photo.
Fail: top is regenerated or materially restaged.

## T2 — Composition match
Pass: lower artwork preserves the main source geometry and subject placement.
Fail: lower half becomes an unrelated scene.

## T3 — Travel mixed-media
Pass: ink line + dry brush + watercolor / collage feel with 4–6 controlled spot colors.
Fail: generic glossy illustration or photorealistic repaint.

## T4 — East Asian ink
Pass: xuan-paper, broken ink, flying white, negative space, sparse ink dots.
Fail: dense decorative pseudo-traditional styling.

## T5 — Person fidelity
Pass: age, hairstyle, clothing, pose and recognizable identity remain consistent.
Fail: person becomes a generic character.

## T6 — Single-element revision
Input: "下面颜色少一点".
Pass: lower palette changes only.
Fail: top photo, layout, or person changes.

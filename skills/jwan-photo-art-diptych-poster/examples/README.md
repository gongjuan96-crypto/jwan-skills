# Example briefs

## Example A — Travel architecture
One city or travel photo. Preserve the top photo. Lower half uses ink line, dry brush, watercolor blocks, four to six spot colors and light torn-paper texture.

## Example B — Contemporary ink landscape
One landscape photo. Preserve top. Lower half simplifies the same scene into xuan paper, broken ink, flying white and large blank zones.

## Example C — Portrait in environment
One person within a travel scene. Preserve identity and clothing. Bottom interpretation keeps the same position and proportion instead of enlarging the person into a portrait.

# Jwan Fisheye City Cover

Create a vertical 9:16 editorial cover: preserve the supplied city or street photo in the upper half, and create a centered fisheye-inspired illustrated portrait in the lower half. Keep the lower portrait small with generous white space, preserve the subject's identity, and use a coherent city-cover palette. Add a small natural `Jwan` signature only when requested. Avoid unrelated text, watermarks, extra characters, distorted faces, and oversized lower artwork.

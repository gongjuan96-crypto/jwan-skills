# Jwan Perspective Crowd Editorial Poster

Create a vertical 9:16 editorial advertising image built around spatial depth rather than a flat lineup of people.

## Choose one depth mechanism

Use one main compositional system:
- nested frames
- converging perspective
- stacked levels
- layered slabs

Do not combine all four mechanically. Pick the one that best creates a clear spatial hierarchy.

## Human count and distribution

Default: 6–8 adults for a large scene. Reduce to fewer people when the space cannot support believable depth.

Required:
- distinct foreground, midground, and background figures
- natural size reduction with distance
- irregular spacing
- overlapping bodies and architecture
- some figures partially hidden behind frames, walls, platforms, or each other
- varied pose and orientation

Forbidden:
- evenly spaced people
- grid layout
- same-size figures
- group-photo lineup
- identical pose repetition

## Architectural behavior

Use large geometric masses to create structure:
- walls
- portals
- frames
- platforms
- slabs
- corridors
- stepped levels

People should pass through, around, behind, or across these structures. Architecture must participate in the composition instead of serving as a decorative background.

## Camera and perspective

- Use one camera position and coherent vanishing logic.
- Low, eye-level, high-angle, or oblique views are allowed.
- When changing camera angle, rebuild all human scale and occlusion relationships consistently.
- Avoid lens effects that destroy legibility unless explicitly requested.

## Editorial hierarchy

- One dominant visual gesture.
- Strong contrast and clean architectural space.
- Large negative-space zones where possible.
- Main title concise; secondary copy small.
- Image must read as an advertisement/editorial image, not a 3D crowd simulation screenshot.
- Small handwritten Jwan signature bottom-right.

## Identity-reference behavior

If reference portraits are supplied, preserve recognizable facial identity, apparent age, hairstyle, clothing cues, and overall character as accurately as possible while allowing pose and depth placement to vary.

Do not clone the same face repeatedly unless the brief intentionally calls for the same person in multiple positions.

## Revision discipline

- "换 pose" -> change poses while preserving depth system.
- "换配色" -> preserve composition.
- "换角度" -> rebuild perspective consistently.
- "人数减少" -> remove figures while preserving front/mid/back rhythm.
- "人物换位置" -> re-balance depth and occlusion, not a flat shuffle.

## Hard avoid list

- regular grid
- equal spacing
- all figures on one depth plane
- all faces directed toward camera
- group-photo stance
- architecture used only as a backdrop
- inconsistent human scale
- excessive decorative objects
- crowded text blocks

## Quality gate

Verify:
- 9:16.
- Chosen depth mechanism is clear.
- Foreground/midground/background are all readable.
- Figure scale changes plausibly with depth.
- At least two meaningful occlusion relationships exist.
- Human placement is irregular rather than evenly distributed.
- Architecture actively shapes movement and depth.
- Main composition remains clean enough for editorial use.
- Jwan signature appears bottom-right.

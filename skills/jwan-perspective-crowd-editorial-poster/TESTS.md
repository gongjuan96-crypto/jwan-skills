# Test checklist — jwan-perspective-crowd-editorial-poster

## T1 — Three depth layers
Pass: foreground, midground, background are all visibly different.
Fail: all people occupy one plane.

## T2 — Scale
Pass: farther people are naturally smaller.
Fail: same-size figures despite distance.

## T3 — Irregular placement
Pass: asymmetric spacing and pose variation.
Fail: grid, row, or group-photo formation.

## T4 — Occlusion
Pass: at least two meaningful person/architecture or person/person overlaps.
Fail: every body is isolated.

## T5 — Reduced crowd
Input: reduce from 8 to 4 people.
Pass: depth rhythm survives.
Fail: remaining figures become evenly spaced.

## T6 — Angle change
Pass: all scale, vanishing, and occlusion relationships rebuild coherently.
Fail: old figures remain pasted into new camera view.

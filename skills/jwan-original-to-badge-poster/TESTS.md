# Test checklist — jwan-original-to-badge-poster

Version tested: 1.0.0

## T1 — Exact split
Pass: top and bottom each occupy exactly half the 9:16 canvas.
Fail: visible 60/40 or other split.

## T2 — Original-photo lock
Pass: upper half remains recognizably the supplied photo with only minor level cleanup.
Fail: scene, face, clothing, background, or subject count is regenerated.

## T3 — Badge hierarchy
Pass: lower collectible is centered and smaller than its field.
Fail: it fills the entire lower half or becomes a second full illustration.

## T4 — Scene-led source
For a landscape/event photo with small people:
Pass: environment remains primary and figures remain proportionally small.
Fail: distant figures become oversized portraits.

## T5 — No unrequested text
Pass: no title frame/ribbon/name/age appears by default.
Fail: generated commemorative copy appears without request.

## T6 — Single-element revision
Input: "徽章再小一点".
Pass: only lower badge scale changes.
Fail: upper photo or badge style changes.

## T7 — Signature
Pass: small Jwan signature bottom-right.

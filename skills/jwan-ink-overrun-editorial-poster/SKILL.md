# Jwan Ink Overrun Editorial Poster

Create one independent 3:4 poster. Keep the original photograph in the upper half with identity, pose, light, and atmosphere intact. Rebuild the lower half as a minimal black-and-white hand-drawn editorial illustration inside a black rectangle, allowing the most recognizable contour or object to deliberately break the boundary. Use sparse gray blocks and a short image-related English title plus notes. Preserve generous white space. Do not make a collage, add unrelated text, cover the face or hands, add a watermark, or output multiple designs together.

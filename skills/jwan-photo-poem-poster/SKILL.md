# Jwan Photo Poem Poster

Create one finished poster from one supplied photo. Treat the photo and its illustrated counterpart as a single visual memory rather than a multi-photo collage.

## Required output

- Return one complete poster, not separate panels or a contact sheet.
- Keep the supplied subject recognizable: preserve identity, pose, clothing cues, important objects, and the emotional gesture.
- Pair the photo with a restrained hand-drawn or painterly illustration derived from the same subject, action, or memory.
- Keep the composition calm and spacious. Use a clear image area and a dedicated caption area below it; do not place copy across faces or important details.
- Use a warm editorial palette and tactile paper, ink, crayon, or watercolor details only when they support the source photo.

## Caption behavior

- Place the main Chinese caption below the image when the user requests the established chat style.
- Write one short, natural sentence that reflects the visible mood or action. Do not invent names, dates, locations, or facts.
- Add one small English companion line when useful; keep it secondary and concise.
- If the user supplies exact copy, reproduce it exactly unless they request editing.
- Example tone: “分享一点甜，也分享今天” with a small English companion such as “A little sweetness to share.”

## Composition

1. Identify the strongest subject and emotional action in the source photo.
2. Preserve the photo as the anchor and build the illustration as a visual echo, not a replacement.
3. Use a clean transition between photo and illustration or a restrained paper overlap; avoid arbitrary decorative clutter.
4. Reserve generous negative space for the caption and keep type visually secondary.
5. Check that the final image reads as one coherent memory poster at social-sharing size.

## Avoid

- Do not combine unrelated source photos into one crowded scene.
- Do not redraw the subject into an unrelated character or alter identity.
- Do not cover faces, hands, or the main object with typography or decoration.
- Do not use long paragraphs, pseudo-text, watermarks, logos, or unreadable generated lettering.

## Quality gate

Before returning the poster, verify that the source subject remains recognizable, the illustration clearly echoes the source, the caption is legible and short, and the lower text area has enough breathing room.

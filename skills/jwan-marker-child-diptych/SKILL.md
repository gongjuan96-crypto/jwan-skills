# Jwan Marker Child Diptych

Create one finished vertical 9:16 diptych from a supplied photo.

- Preserve the original photo in the upper half, including identity, pose, clothing, lighting, and relevant setting.
- Redraw the same subject in the lower half as a marker-and-child-doodle illustration, centered with generous white space and occupying about half of the lower panel.
- Use lively hand-drawn marker contours, simple color blocks, and recognizable source-specific clothing and pose.
- Add a small handwritten `Jwan` signature only when requested.
- Do not add extra people, logos, watermarks, unrelated copy, sticker-sheet clutter, or a photorealistic lower panel.
- Check facial identity, pose continuity, panel split, margins, and unintended text before delivery.

Source inspiration: Wibi Style `marker-child-doodle`; this Skill is a Jwan-specific adaptation.

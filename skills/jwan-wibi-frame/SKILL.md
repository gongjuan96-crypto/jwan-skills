# Jwan Wibi Frame

Use one supplied photo to create a finished vertical 9:16 diptych.

- Keep the original photo realistic in the upper half. Preserve identity, pose, clothing, lighting, and important background context.
- Create a small, centered retro-comic avatar of the same subject in the lower half. Keep generous white space; the avatar should not dominate the panel.
- Use simple ink contours, restrained flat colors, and an editorial framed-comic feeling.
- Add a small natural handwritten `Jwan` signature only when requested.
- Do not add unrelated characters, logos, watermarks, stickers, or unrelated text.
- Check that the lower avatar remains recognizably connected to the upper photo and that the face is not covered or distorted.

Source inspiration: Wibi Style `wibi-frame`; this Skill is a Jwan-specific adaptation.

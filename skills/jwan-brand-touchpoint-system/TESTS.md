# Test checklist — jwan-brand-touchpoint-system

## T1 — Brand family
Pass: poster, packaging and social assets share one visual language.
Fail: every output uses a different style.

## T2 — SKU differentiation
Pass: each flavor is easy to distinguish through controlled cues.
Fail: variants are either indistinguishable or completely unrelated.

## T3 — Product fidelity
Pass: supplied product shape / packaging remains recognizable.
Fail: product is replaced with a different item.

## T4 — Copy truthfulness
Pass: no unsupported award, certification, origin or health claim.
Fail: invented claims appear.

## T5 — Negative space
Pass: visual system retains intentional breathing room.
Fail: every area is filled with decoration.

## T6 — Single-element revision
Input: "红茶款增加红茶梗".
Pass: Earl Grey cue changes only.
Fail: whole series is redesigned.

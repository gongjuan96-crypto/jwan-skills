# Test checklist — jwan-fashion-identity-triptych-poster

Version tested: 1.0.0

## T1 — Three panels
Pass: three tall dominant panels.
Fail: collage with more/less than three dominant portrait panels.

## T2 — Angle logic
Pass: left profile / front / opposite profile.
Fail: three nearly identical angles.

## T3 — Identity
Pass: same recognizable person throughout.
Fail: panels look like different models.

## T4 — Age and gender stability
Pass: apparent age and gender presentation remain consistent.
Fail: one panel noticeably de-ages or re-genders the subject.

## T5 — Styling variation
Pass: controlled variation in hats, glasses or accessories.
Fail: unrelated costume characters.

## T6 — Single-element revision
Input: remove sunglasses.
Pass: eyewear changes only.
Fail: face, hairstyle, wardrobe and panel layout drift.

# Jwan Fashion Identity Triptych Poster

Create one vertical 9:16 high-fashion editorial portrait using one identity reference. The same person appears in three tall vertical panels, each with a different camera angle.

## Core panel system

Use three tall vertical panels:
1. left side profile
2. front-facing portrait
3. opposite side profile

The three images must read as the same person photographed in one coherent editorial series.

## Identity fidelity

Treat identity as the highest-priority constraint.

Preserve as accurately as the generation system allows:
- facial structure
- apparent age
- gender presentation
- hairstyle / hairline
- eyebrow shape
- eye shape
- nose
- lips
- jawline
- skin tone
- distinctive facial hair or accessories
- overall individual character

Do not beautify into a different person.
Do not de-age, re-gender, or change ethnicity.
Do not replace distinctive facial structure with a generic fashion model.

## Styling variation

Each panel may use different:
- headwear
- sunglasses / eyewear
- subtle accessories
- micro-expression
- crop or head angle

Styling must feel naturally adapted to the referenced person's age, gender presentation, and fashion character.

Avoid costume gimmicks unless explicitly requested.

## Visual direction

Default:
- warm off-white or light neutral background
- luxury magazine aesthetic
- soft studio lighting
- realistic skin texture
- clean geometric composition
- restrained palette
- minimal secondary text
- no excessive beauty retouching
- small Jwan signature bottom-right

Panels may be equal width or slightly asymmetrical, but the triptych must remain clean and intentional.

## Continuity

Across all three panels:
- keep skin tone consistent
- keep hair length / hairline consistent unless headwear naturally hides it
- keep wardrobe family coherent
- keep lighting family consistent
- avoid three unrelated photographic styles

## Revision discipline

- "换帽子" -> change headwear only
- "去掉墨镜" -> eyewear only
- "背景更白一点" -> background only
- "中间正脸更大" -> adjust center crop only
- "人物不像" -> prioritize identity correction over styling novelty

## Hard avoid list

- three different people
- large age drift
- inconsistent hairline
- gender drift
- over-smoothed plastic skin
- unrelated costumes
- heavy text overlays
- random panel widths without compositional logic
- changing identity during a styling-only revision

## Quality gate

Verify:
- 9:16
- exactly three dominant vertical portrait panels
- left / front / opposite-profile logic is readable
- same identity across all panels
- age and gender presentation remain stable
- styling differences are controlled
- background and lighting form one series
- skin texture remains realistic
- Jwan signature appears bottom-right

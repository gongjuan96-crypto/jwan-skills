# Jwan Pressure Crowd Editorial Poster

Create one vertical 9:16 editorial advertising / satire poster built around a single visual idea: one central protagonist is isolated inside a dense crowd whose gestures create social pressure, conflicting feedback, noise, or overload.

The original design use case is a designer surrounded by too many people giving contradictory feedback, but the system can adapt to other professional or social-pressure scenarios.

## Narrative structure

The image must communicate the conflict without requiring long explanatory copy.

Required:
- one clearly identifiable central protagonist
- a much denser surrounding crowd
- conflicting hand gestures or directions
- visual isolation of the protagonist despite physical crowding
- layered body overlap
- strong emotional contrast between center and crowd

## Default designer scenario

When used for design-feedback satire:
- central designer sits with back or three-quarter back toward the viewer
- designer works at a desk or large monitor
- screen shows a believable graphic-design layout / editing interface
- crowd gathers from multiple directions
- crowd points at the screen, gestures, argues, raises hands, or gives contradictory instructions
- designer remains the visual center even when the crowd is extremely dense

## Crowd density

The crowd should feel excessive.

Use:
- foreground heads / shoulders partially entering frame
- midground faces and hands
- background bodies compressed by perspective
- overlap and occlusion
- irregular body scale
- different expressions and gesture directions

Avoid:
- neat semicircle with even spacing
- identical expressions
- repeated cloned faces
- equal-size people
- empty gaps that weaken the feeling of overload

If the user asks for "many, many, many" people, increase perceived density through layered overlap and depth rather than simply shrinking dozens of identical figures into a grid.

## Camera and composition

- 9:16 vertical
- central protagonist anchored near the lower-middle or central axis
- crowd may form an arc, enclosure, funnel, or compressed wall around the protagonist
- preserve one readable visual hierarchy: center first, crowd pressure second, interface / prop third
- use cinematic depth rather than flat montage

## Lighting and color

Default visual language:
- dramatic editorial photography
- strong side or top-side lighting
- deep shadows
- bright facial highlights
- realistic skin texture
- high contrast
- documentary / theatrical atmosphere

Color modes:
- monochrome high-contrast black-and-white
- normal realistic color
- restricted editorial color palette when explicitly requested

When changing from monochrome to color, preserve composition and gesture logic.

## Gesture vocabulary

Use a varied combination:
- pointing toward screen/object
- raised index finger
- open palm disagreement
- hands from off-frame
- leaning over shoulders
- speaking toward center
- clenched fist for emphasis
- conflicting directional gestures

Do not make every person angry. Mix frustration, certainty, confusion, urgency, insistence and unsolicited confidence.

## Text hierarchy

If a headline is supplied:
- keep it clearly readable
- leave sufficient breathing room
- do not let crowd heads cover critical headline text unless intentionally designed
- secondary interface overlays or captions remain subordinate

## Identity reference

If a central person reference is supplied:
- preserve recognizable identity, apparent age, hairstyle, clothing cues and body character as accurately as possible
- keep the central person visually distinct from the surrounding crowd

## Revision discipline

- "人数增加10倍" -> increase perceived crowd density and layering while preserving the central protagonist and camera.
- "改正常色彩" -> change color treatment only.
- "删掉文字" -> remove text only.
- "设计师人物参考这张" -> replace central identity while preserving crowd logic.
- "周围再挤一点" -> increase occlusion and compression, not regular grid density.

## Hard avoid list

- evenly spaced crowd
- group-photo staging
- cloned repeated faces
- crowd becoming more visually important than the protagonist
- central protagonist disappearing
- generic office stock-photo look
- fake unreadable giant text blocks on the monitor
- inconsistent scale / perspective
- changing camera during color-only revision

## Quality gate

Verify:
- 9:16
- central protagonist is immediately identifiable
- crowd feels excessive and layered
- gestures conflict rather than repeat
- foreground / midground / background all contribute to density
- protagonist remains visually isolated
- composition remains readable at poster scale
- requested color mode is respected
- small Jwan signature appears bottom-right

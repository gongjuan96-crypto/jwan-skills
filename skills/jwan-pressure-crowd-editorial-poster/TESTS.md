# Test checklist — jwan-pressure-crowd-editorial-poster

Version tested: 1.0.0

## T1 — Central protagonist
Pass: one person is clearly the narrative center.
Fail: crowd overwhelms the image until the protagonist is lost.

## T2 — Density
Pass: crowd feels excessive through overlap, multiple depths and off-frame bodies.
Fail: neat evenly spaced semicircle.

## T3 — Gesture conflict
Pass: gestures point in different directions and express varied forms of pressure.
Fail: repeated identical pointing poses.

## T4 — Perspective
Pass: foreground heads/hands, midground faces and background bodies show natural scale changes.
Fail: all figures same size.

## T5 — Color-only revision
Input: change monochrome to normal color.
Pass: composition and gestures remain stable.
Fail: crowd arrangement or camera changes.

## T6 — Identity replacement
Pass: central referenced person changes while the pressure composition remains.
Fail: crowd system collapses or reference identity appears in multiple cloned bodies.

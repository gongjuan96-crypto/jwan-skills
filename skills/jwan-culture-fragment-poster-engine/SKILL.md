# Jwan Culture Fragment Poster Engine

Convert fragmented cultural references into one coherent poster system. Identify the place, object, person, memory, and emotional anchor; establish a clear hierarchy; preserve the supplied subject's identity and material cues; and combine photo, restrained illustration, typography, and negative space without turning the result into a random scrapbook.

Use source-specific motifs, controlled color, a readable title, and a deliberate focal point. Keep the output as one finished poster, avoid unrelated decorative elements and pseudo-text, and check cultural references, anatomy, hierarchy, margins, and legibility before delivery.
